How it Works

Thank you for participating in the 2026-7 season! We are addressing key information gaps in the Wisconsin Cover Crop Data Network dataset to better capture the true impact of cover crops on Wisconsin farms. We would like to track soil test results, cash crop yield data, as well as cover crop tissue sample data. You will also work with us to schedule a time for a soil sample in the fall and again in the spring. In return, we send you $150 honorarium along with your personal biomass production and nutrient quality analyses for that cover crop. We also release regular project reports on statewide findings and maintain our interactive cover crop dashboard. As a commitment to your privacy we promise that all field data we gather will never be made public in association with any personal information without your consent. Your participation is voluntary and you can leave the project at any time. If all the above is agreeable to you, please proceed and welcome!

Don’t have an account? Please email mingram@michaelfields.org.

Reach out to Dan Marzu at dan.marzu@wisc.edu or Mrill Ingram at mingram@wisc.edu with any questions.

If you have any trouble registering or would like assistance registering please email Mrill Ingram at mingram@wisc.edu.
