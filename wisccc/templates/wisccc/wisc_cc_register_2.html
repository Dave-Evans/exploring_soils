How it works

Thank you for participating in the 2026-7 season! We are addressing key information gaps in the Wisconsin Cover Crop Data Network dataset to better capture the true impact of cover crops on Wisconsin farms. We would like to track soil test results, cash crop yield data, as well as cover crop tissue sample data.

We provide you with a free sampling kit and addressed/stamped mailers so you can send your plant tissue samples to labs for analysis. We would also like to gather soil samples from this sampled field, one in fall 2026 and one in spring 2027. We will reach out to schedule a convenient time to gather soil samples. In return, we'll send you $150 honorarium along with your personal soil test results, biomass production and nutrient quality analyses for the cover crop and spring/fall soil test results for the field.
